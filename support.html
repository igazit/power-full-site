Power-Full Support

Requires macOS 14 or later.

Need help or found a bug? Open an issue on the support tracker. Please include your Mac model, macOS version and what you expected to see.

Getting started

Power-Full lives in the menu bar and has no Dock icon or window. Click the battery item to open the details; right-click it for About and Quit. Use the gear in the popover to choose what the menu bar shows (percentage, charger input, system usage, battery flow), pick a temperature unit, or open Power-Full at login.

Frequently asked questions

Why does it say “Plugged In, Draining”?

Your Mac is using more power than the charger can supply, so the battery makes up the difference. Use a higher-wattage charger, or reduce the load (screen brightness, heavy apps).

Why is there no CPU or fan temperature?

Power-Full runs inside the macOS App Sandbox and only uses public system APIs. It shows the battery temperature and the system's thermal state. CPU, GPU and fan sensors are not available to sandboxed apps.

I have a Mac mini, iMac, Mac Studio or Mac Pro

These Macs have no battery, so Power-Full shows a short explanation instead of data.

The menu bar item is missing

On Macs with a notch, a crowded menu bar can hide items. Quit a few other menu bar apps, or launch Power-Full again from Applications. It will restore its item and open the popover.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. See the privacy policy.
