Privacy Policy

Power-Full for macOS · Effective November 25, 2026

Power-Full does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data.

What the app reads

Power-Full reads battery and charger information that your Mac already provides: charge level, power in watts, temperature, capacity, cycle count and thermal state. This information is read locally, shown on screen and discarded. A short in-memory history (the last five minutes) drives the graph and is never written to disk.

What the app stores

Only your own preferences (what the menu bar shows, temperature unit) are saved on your Mac in the app's sandbox container. Nothing else is stored.

Network, accounts and tracking

Power-Full has no network access, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. It does not track you.

Children

Power-Full collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
